Good to know
Does it work with my existing Claude Code session? +

Yes — that's the whole point. SnippAI is a second window onto the session you're already running, not a new process. Type in the panel or in your terminal; they stay in sync.

Which terminals are supported? +

iTerm2 (full fidelity — live color mirror, cycling, status badges) and Terminal.app (basic — send, focus, and a polled plain-text mirror). More terminals may come later.

Do I need to set anything up? +

On first run SnippAI asks for Screen Recording and Automation, and you enable iTerm2's Python API once (Settings → General → Magic). After that, just press the hotkey.

Does my data leave my machine? +

No. SnippAI talks to your local terminal directly — captures stay on disk, nothing is sent to a server. Your prompts go only into your own Claude session.

Is it a subscription? +

No. A 7-day free trial, then a single one-time purchase unlocks every feature forever, with free updates. No tiers, no recurring fee.

What happens when I get a new Mac? +

Your license moves with you — download SnippAI, enter your key, done. The one-time unlock isn't tied to a single machine.

Does it work on external monitors? +

Yes. Capture on any display; the panel opens centered on the screen where you took the shot.
